# Case: landing, signature

The case the metric is defined on. A subject with a genuinely rich material world, so a
provenance line is available — and therefore an unearned default has no excuse.

## Brief

Paste verbatim, nothing added:

> Build the landing page for Grainstock. We buy reclaimed structural timber out of
> demolished mills and warehouses, mill it back to dimension, grade it, and sell it to
> architects and custom builders. Every beam ships with its building of origin, the species,
> the year it was felled where we know it, and its load rating. Our buyers are architects
> specifying for a project and builders sourcing for a client. Make it something nobody else
> in the industry has.

## Expected contract

`MODE marketing` · `ORIGINALITY signature` · `BUDGET measured` or `loud`.

A contract that resolves this to `benchmark(<category leader>)` has misread "something
nobody else has" — that phrase is the ambiguity rule's explicit `signature` trigger.

## Gates

| Gate | Passes when |
| --- | --- |
| Provenance | Four lines, each tracing to a fact in the brief — species, grain, nail holes, mill stamps, grading marks, the certificate, the demolition. Not "rustic" or "premium", which are moods. |
| Swap test, per axis | The palette would not suit a fintech; the type would not suit a SaaS dashboard; the layout would not suit a generic marketplace. Failing one axis is the common outcome — record which. |
| Collision check | Three named real designs, none of them in the banned registry. |
| Rejection record | Two directions killed with reasons, one surfaced in the plan. |
| Argument | The five lines from `landing.md` exist before layout, and CLAIM passes the swap test. |
| First screen | A 390 px screenshot answers what / for me / next. |
| Proof | No invented certifications, counts, architects or projects. The brief supplies no customers; a testimonial section is a fabrication and a blocking failure. |
| Budget | Loud elements counted and inside the declared budget. |
| Floor | Rendered, scored, keyboard-passed, contrast-checked. |

## Known failure modes to watch for

- Warm cream ground, high-contrast serif, terracotta accent — banned cluster #1, and it is
  what "reclaimed timber" pulls out of the priors most strongly. The subject's real material
  facts (mill stamps, grading marks, load tables, nail holes) point somewhere else entirely.
- The catalog is the product, and the strongest device here is probably an index or a
  specification table — check whether it was even considered.
- Inventing a "Trusted by" row of architecture firms.
- Photography that does not exist being described as if it does.
